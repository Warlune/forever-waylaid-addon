## 0.9.10 — Compass map zoom

- Added small Classic-style + and − buttons to the compass map.
- Step between world, continent and the current zone or city using the client map hierarchy, including Forever zones.
- Keep the selected zoom level through route refreshes and follow the player when changing zones.
- Show the next map name on hover and disable buttons at the zoom limits.

Reload after installing; the addon should report v0.9.10. This remains a preview release.
