## 0.9.9 — Forever boats and skycutters

- Added Stormwind Harbor–Auberdine and the neutral Steamwheedle Port–Powderfuse Port (Riverglades) boat.
- Replaced the old Menethil–Auberdine pair with the directed Menethil → Southshore → Auberdine → Menethil cycle. Stay-aboard journeys include intermediate dock time and pay one boarding wait.
- Added Skywatcher Plateau–Valanaar for Horde and Dalaran–Valanaar for Alliance Skyborne. Conservative access filters avoid recommending hostile capital docks.
- Refreshed boat/zeppelin approaches and approximate directional times from published Forever data. These are estimates, not live schedules or measured trip times.
- Added intermediate ports to map lines, plus stay-aboard directions and estimated boarding waits in delivery details.
- Fixed route lines disappearing when their destination is beyond the current city/zone map edge.

Full journeys still need in-game testing. Lines remain schematic: the global road network around walls and mountains is not implemented. Retained tram approaches and mage landing points require expanded-map verification. No special Forever teleports have been added.

Reload after installing; the addon should report v0.9.9. Releases remain on 0.x until the project owner authorizes 1.0.
