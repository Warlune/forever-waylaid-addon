## 0.9.8 — Cross-continent delivery routes

- Fixed the compass dropping cross-continent deliveries: routes now connect your current position to the customer through known flights and Classic public transport.
- Added faction-appropriate boats, zeppelins and the Deeprun Tram, with boarding-point directions and transport pins/lines on the maps.
- Added cooldown-aware Hearthstone, owned Engineering transporters and learned mage capital teleports/portals with class, skill, specialization and reagent checks.
- Learn Hearthstone coordinates when binding or using it; changed bindings invalidate stale coordinates.
- Warn in the compass and flight tooltip when Eastern Kingdoms or Kalimdor is not scanned and the route may not be optimal.
- Reserve personal travel resources when planning multiple deliveries. No warlock summons or Forever-specific teleports.

Travel times and map lines remain estimates, not terrain-aware road navigation or live transport schedules. Full boat, flight and teleport journeys still need player testing in Forever.

Reload after installing; the addon should report v0.9.8. Releases remain on 0.x until the project owner authorizes 1.0.
